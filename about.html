Professional focus

I am a customer experience, programme and operations leader with more than 15 years of experience across international development, diplomatic environments, security services and telecommunications. My work has included strategic partnerships, digital transformation, service operations, contact centres, CRM architecture, business-process redesign and AI-enabled public-sector initiatives.

At the British High Commission, I progressed from leading the Customer Help Desk to managing Office for National Statistics partnerships with the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics and statistical institutions in Somalia. That work connected delivery planning, stakeholder management, international collaboration, budgets, data capability and emerging AI use cases.

Earlier roles at GardaWorld / KK Security, Telkom Kenya and KenCall built the operational foundation: managing distributed teams, improving service levels, redesigning support structures, migrating systems and translating customer feedback into process and product change.

How I approach transformation

Start with the journey

Map the real experience first, including hand-offs, delays, duplicated effort and unclear ownership.

Make the operating model visible

Translate assumptions into workflows, responsibilities, service levels, risks and measurable outcomes.

Use technology as leverage

CRM, digital services, automation and AI are most useful when they remove friction rather than add another layer.

Close the feedback loop

Operational metrics, user feedback and stakeholder review should continuously shape the next iteration.
