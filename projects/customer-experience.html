Case study

British High Commission Customer Experience Transformation

A seven-year operating-model transformation across onboarding, service levels, CRM and internal customer experience.

Role
Head of Customer Help Desk
Period
2015 – 2022
Sector
Diplomatic / Corporate services
Focus
CX · CRM · Operations · Service design
Overview

The Customer Help Desk sat at the centre of corporate services for a diplomatic mission, connecting staff and dependants with IT, Finance, Procurement, HR, Estates and other service teams. The role became a platform for redesigning how those services were requested, tracked, measured and improved.

← All case studies

The context

When customer journeys cross multiple departments, friction accumulates at the hand-offs: unclear ownership, slow routing, inconsistent communication and weak visibility. The transformation focused on making those journeys more predictable while improving both customer confidence and internal throughput.

What the work involved

  • Re-engineered onboarding and induction for arriving diplomats, dependants and country-based staff.
  • Mapped cross-functional corporate-service processes with IT, Finance, Procurement, HR and Estates to identify bottlenecks.
  • Managed service-level performance across digital and telephonic channels.
  • Implemented customer-feedback loops and used survey insights to improve the end-to-end journey.
  • Redesigned CRM architecture to improve ticket tracking accuracy, customer effort and help-desk throughput.
  • Led service continuity during the COVID-19 pandemic and supported staff welfare.
  • Supported high-level diplomatic visit logistics with corporate and security requirements.

Selected outcomes

98%onboarding completed within 72 hours
92%customer satisfaction score
90%responses within 24 hours

Transferable value

The strongest lesson is that customer experience is an operating-system problem. Improving it required process ownership, data visibility, cross-functional relationships and frontline empathy at the same time.
