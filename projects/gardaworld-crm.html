Case study

GardaWorld Customer Operations & CRM

National customer-service operations redesigned through process mapping, CRM, data quality and contact-centre structure.

Role
Customer Service Manager
Period
2013 – 2015
Sector
Security services
Focus
CRM · Contact centre · ISO · Data quality
Overview

At KK Security Services, a GardaWorld company, the customer-service function spanned seven branches and depended on accurate records, consistent processes and coordinated work across Sales, Marketing and service teams.

← All case studies

The context

Distributed service teams amplify inconsistency. The work therefore addressed both the operating model and the information layer: how processes were documented, how work entered the contact centre, how CRM supported departments and how reliable customer data was.

What the work involved

  • Managed 14 customer-service officers across seven branches and coached performance against KPIs.
  • Mapped customer-service processes for ISO certification.
  • Created a customer-service playbook to improve onboarding and cross-department training.
  • Delivered a Microsoft Dynamics CRM project supporting Sales, Customer Service and Marketing.
  • Designed the contact-centre setup and managed service-level and handling-time performance.
  • Improved customer-record data integrity to strengthen both service and debt collection.

Selected outcomes

65% → 3%customer-record error rate
14customer-service officers across 7 branches
3m 30saverage handling time

Transferable value

The transformation demonstrates how process design, CRM and data quality reinforce each other. A contact centre becomes dramatically more effective when its operating rules and underlying information are both trustworthy.
