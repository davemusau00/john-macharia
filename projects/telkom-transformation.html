Case study

Telkom Kenya Service & Network Change

Customer-support restructuring alongside major telecom infrastructure and service-change programmes.

Role
Customer Care Team Manager
Period
2009 – 2013
Sector
Telecommunications
Focus
Change · Support · Networks · Operations
Overview

The role combined second-level customer support with organisational and technology change during a period of major transformation at Telkom Kenya. It required keeping day-to-day service stable while new structures and network capabilities were introduced.

← All case studies

The context

Large telecom change programmes are felt by customers before they are fully understood internally. The customer-care function therefore had to absorb escalations, consolidate support teams and feed trend information back into product and technical teams while migrations and rollouts were under way.

What the work involved

  • Managed a 20-person back-office team handling escalated voice and data cases.
  • Merged three support sections into one core team for improved structural efficiency.
  • Collaborated with product managers and third-level support using feedback and trend analysis.
  • Supported Avaya contact-centre setup.
  • Supported Intelligent Network migration from Ericsson to ZTE.
  • Participated in Project Cheetah, converting traditional landlines to a CDMA network.
  • Supported the 3G network rollout programme.

Selected outcomes

20staff in second-level support
3 → 1support sections consolidated
4major change initiatives supported

Transferable value

The experience built a durable change-management perspective: transformation has to be designed for the frontline and customer-facing teams who will absorb uncertainty while the new system is still becoming normal.
