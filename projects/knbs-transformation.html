Case study

KNBS × ONS Partnership & Digital Transformation

International programme leadership connecting statistical capability, digital public services and AI adoption.

Role
Programme Partnership Manager
Period
2022 – Jul 2026
Sector
Public sector / International development
Focus
Programme · AI · Data · Digital
Overview

This role connected the UK Office for National Statistics with the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics and statistical partners in Somalia. The work combined partnership management, PMO disciplines, stakeholder coordination, digital transformation, capacity building and budget oversight.

← All case studies

The context

National statistical institutions operate inside complex stakeholder environments. Technical priorities, international partners, funding rules, public access to data and institutional capability all have to move together. The programme therefore required more than project tracking: it needed alignment between strategy, delivery, partnerships and user-facing digital services.

What the work involved

  • Managed the strategic partnership between KNBS and ONS, aligning cross-organisational goals around national statistical standards and methodologies.
  • Coordinated international stakeholders including the World Bank, Stats Sweden, GPSDD and UN agencies across Kenya and Somalia initiatives.
  • Drove data and AI capacity-building programmes across Kenyan Ministries, Departments and Agencies.
  • Directed digital-transformation work including the KNBS website and Stats-Chat AI-driven bot.
  • Supported the national AI Challenge and emerging-technology integration within a government context.
  • Applied PMO practices around planning, risk, change control, benefits realisation, concept notes and business cases.
  • Monitored programme expenditure of approximately £100K per year with attention to ODA compliance.
  • Supported delivery of the 2026 Kenya Data Festival through milestones, stakeholder engagement and logistics.

Transferable value

The transferable capability is orchestration: creating enough structure for multiple institutions, priorities and delivery streams to move in the same direction without losing sight of outcomes.
